## 1. Admission levels

| Level | Meaning | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference-only | Study information architecture or interaction idea | Niuva need, provenance, no copied identity |
| Local adaptation | Recreate a bounded mechanic with native/CSS/current APIs | Exact consumer, accessibility, reduced motion, rollback |
| Runtime dependency | Add a package/library to source | Concrete need, license, bundle, owner, maintenance, fallback, exact-file approval |

## 3. Admission checklist for a future consumer

1. Name the route/component and exact problem solved.
2. Specify reference-only, local adaptation, or runtime dependency.
3. Record source/provenance, license, bundle/runtime cost, owner, and removal
   plan.
4. Fill NDS 13 fields, including keyboard, screen-reader, responsive, locale,
   reduced-motion, and complete static fallback.
5. Prove at least two same-meaning consumers before promotion; one page with
   repeated selectors is one consumer context.
6. Obtain exact-file, dependency, and delivery gates separately.
